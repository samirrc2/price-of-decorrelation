Reviewer #3, Concern #2

Reviewer comment:

Recompute uncertainty while accounting for both repeated observations within equities and shared
analysis dates/common market conditions. Justify the bootstrap unit formally, evaluate two-way
equity/date clustering or an appropriate multiway resampling strategy, and regenerate all main and
exploratory confidence intervals accordingly.

Author response:

  - Equity is the pre-registered primary resampling unit because the repeated observations in
    this design are nested within securities: each equity is evaluated on 12 analysis dates, and
    every response for that equity shares company-specific context. Analysis date is the second,
    crossed source of dependence, because all securities evaluated on a given date share common
    market conditions. Neither margin nests inside the other, so they are resampled
    independently rather than hierarchically.
  - The two-way procedure draws equities with replacement and dates with replacement,
    independently, retains the sampled equity-date intersections, and preserves multiplicity on
    both margins: a cluster drawn twice contributes twice. It uses 2,000 draws at the
    pre-registered seed and recomputes the full estimator inside every draw.
  - Primary HOM-HET agreement contrast, Δκ = 0.3363: [0.3035, 0.3689] under equity clustering
    (100 clusters), [0.2989, 0.3656] under date clustering (12 clusters), [0.2786, 0.3877] under
    two-way resampling (1,200 intersections). All exclude zero.
  - The two pre-registered secondary agreement contrasts are measured on the same grid and are
    now reported under the same scheme. HOM-HET-LITE Δκ = 0.249: equity [0.218, 0.280], two-way
    [0.208, 0.290]. HET-LITE-HET Δκ = 0.087: equity [0.067, 0.105], two-way [0.045, 0.123]. Both
    continue to exclude zero.
  - The capability-matched control is measured on the same 100 equities and 12 dates and asserts
    that its interval excludes zero, so it receives the same treatment. Δκ(HOM-SameTier) =
    0.315: equity [0.288, 0.341], two-way [0.270, 0.361]. Both exclude zero.
  - Every exploratory contrast that carries an inferential claim is also reported both ways.
    Error correlation Δφ = 0.454: equity [0.376, 0.534], two-way [0.328, 0.586]. The
    common-subset specification counting HOLD as an error, Δφ = 0.309: equity [0.262, 0.354],
    two-way [0.227, 0.387]. Unanimous-incorrect ratio 1.84: equity [1.42, 2.52], two-way [1.13,
    3.06]. Pile-on Δ = 0.189: equity [0.160, 0.216], two-way [0.140, 0.232]. AURC Δ = -0.001:
    equity [-0.059, 0.060], two-way [-0.113, 0.115]. No conclusion changes under the wider
    scheme: the intervals that excluded the null still exclude it, and the AURC interval still
    contains zero.
  - Two groups of intervals are deliberately not given a two-way treatment, for reasons of
    design rather than convenience. The temperature-sensitivity analysis is a small supporting
    subgrid of eight equities and two analysis dates; two date clusters cannot support
    date-clustered or two-way inference, so its intervals remain clustered on equities only and
    the manuscript now states that this analysis contributes no independent multiway evidence.
    The clinical replication is indexed by MMLU items rather than by equities and dates, so the
    financial equity-date bootstrap does not apply to it; its intervals are clustered on items.
  - The Wilson intervals in the secondary directional-accuracy table are marginal and
    descriptive. They are retained as descriptive only and are not the basis of any inferential
    claim, so replacing them with a clustered interval would imply an inference the manuscript
    does not make.
  - One correction follows from this work rather than from a change of method. The
    implementation used for the robustness table and for the unanimous-incorrect ratio collapsed
    each draw to a set of equities, so an equity drawn twice counted once and each draw used
    only the distinct equities of a 100-draw multiset. That is subsampling, not a cluster
    bootstrap, and it understated the variance. Multiplicity is now preserved, which is why the
    equity-clustered robustness row reproduces the primary interval exactly and why the
    unanimous-incorrect interval is [1.42, 2.52] rather than the narrower [1.51, 2.35]
    previously reported. The resampling code now asserts multiplicity on both margins and fails
    if it is ever lost, so the error cannot recur silently.
